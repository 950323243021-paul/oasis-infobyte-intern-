The process

Three steps, one roll at a time

Every order moves through the darkroom in the same order — there's no shortcut that doesn't cost you a frame.

FRAME 01

Mail it in

Drop your exposed rolls in any padded mailer with your order slip. We text you the moment it lands on our bench.

FRAME 02

We develop it

Hand-processed in small batches — C-41, E-6, or black & white — with fresh chemistry checked every morning against a control strip.

FRAME 03

Scan & return

Every frame gets a flatbed scan at your chosen resolution. Negatives go back to you in archival sleeves, gallery-ready.

What you get

Built for people who shoot, not just snap

SCAN

3400dpi flatbed scans

Every frame, not just the ones you flag — delivered as high-res TIFFs and web-ready JPEGs.

CHEM

Fresh chemistry, every batch

C-41, E-6, and B&W lines are rotated on a fixed schedule and checked with a control strip daily.

NEGS

Negatives always returned

Cut, sleeved in archival PrintFile pages, and mailed back — your film never gets recycled or binned.

TIME

48-hour turnaround

From the bench to your inbox in two business days, with a rush option for the same-day queue.
